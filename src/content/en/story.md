---
title: "Who is Amêndoa D'Cacau?"
description: "A family cocoa house since the nineteenth century. Great-great-grandfather in Bahia, father running Fazenda Eustáquio for 15+ years, Aveiro as the European base."
---

We are a Brazilian house. We make craft chocolate and grow cocoa **without pesticides or chemical fertilisers**. The work is quality beans, selected in several stages, and the tree-to-bar craft — from plant to bar.

We sell the fruit at several stages: selected beans, peeled beans, nibs, chocolate and drinking powder. Everything that leaves the house is meant for kitchens and tables. Europe is reached from Aveiro; the origin is still the Bahian hills.
