The green on our packaging is the forest the cocoa comes from. We grow it **without chemical fertilisers or pesticides**. Beans are selected in several passes — aroma, flavour, size and colour — and we follow the fruit to the bar, the nib, the shell tea and the drinking powder.

There is no shopping cart. If you are a chef, a maker or a distributor in Europe, the way in is a request for a lot or a sample.
