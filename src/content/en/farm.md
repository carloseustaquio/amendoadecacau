---
title: "Where does our cocoa come from?"
description: "Fazenda Eustáquio, southern Bahia, near Jussari and Serra do Teimoso. Around 1000 m, Atlantic Forest, shade-grown, ecological."
---

The cocoa tree is small and needs constant shade. That makes it a partner of the forest: it brings income without asking for the grove to be cut. At Fazenda Eustáquio the plants grow **in the shade of century-old trees** — jequitibás and pink ipês — at an altitude that favours aroma and even ripening.

Growing is **ecological**. No agrochemicals. The fruit that reaches Aveiro is this: forest cocoa, not full-sun monoculture.
